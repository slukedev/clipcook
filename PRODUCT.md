# Product

## Who it's for

Home cooks who save recipes from around the web. Two moments matter:

- **Collecting.** On the couch or at a desk, unhurried. They find a recipe on a site, in a social post, or in a message, and want it saved without the ads and life story, in one or two taps.
- **Cooking.** In the kitchen with a phone propped up, a tablet, or a smart display on the counter. Hands are wet or floury and the screen is read from arm's length. Everything has to be large, high contrast, and reachable without precision.

A household often shares one kitchen display (Echo Show, Nest Hub, an old tablet), and several people cook from the same library.

## What it does

- Clips recipes from links, shares, a bookmarklet, or pasted text, and keeps the source.
- Organizes them with favorites, collections and tags, with instant search.
- Gives a first-time cook a starter set of recipes and an overview of what to gather before step one.
- Cooks one step at a time with timers, scaling, and an awake screen.
- Plans the week's meals, leads with tonight's dish, and shops for the week at the planned servings.
- Builds a grocery list from recipes, merged and grouped by aisle.
- Puts a recipe on a paired kitchen display from the phone, and keeps the display in step with cook mode.
- Lets people own their data: export, import, and self-hosting under the GPL.

## Principles

1. **Content first.** The recipe and its photo carry the page; chrome stays quiet and tonal.
2. **Kitchen-proof.** 48px minimum targets, 56px checklist rows, readable at arm's length, the screen stays on while cooking.
3. **Fast to save.** Clipping starts the moment a link arrives, and a failed clip always offers the next way in.
4. **No dead ends.** Empty, loading, error and offline states each have one clear next step.
5. **Calm copy.** Few words, plain labels, buttons that name the action.

## Platform

Web, installable as a PWA. Phones, tablets, desktops, and smart displays (through a browser or Google Cast).
